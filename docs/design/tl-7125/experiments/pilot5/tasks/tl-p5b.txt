`tl attempt export` has a `hold_cause` column holding the hold's cause as a code, but nothing that says in words why the attempt was held. An experiment reader has to open the database to see the text.

Add the hold's explanation text to the export as a new column named `hold_text`: the same text the dashboard and `tl attempt show` display for that hold, empty (NULL) for an attempt with no hold. It appears in both the CSV and the `--json` output.

Note on checks: the factory runs `just check` and `just test` itself after your answer. They cannot run in your sandbox (no beads database, read-only gate cache), so do not run them; focused pytest runs of the files you touch are fine.

## Acceptance Criteria
- `tl attempt export` and `tl attempt export --json` include `hold_text`, in the column order after `hold_cause`.
- It is NULL (empty in CSV) when the attempt has no hold.
- A focused test covers a held attempt and an attempt that was not held.
